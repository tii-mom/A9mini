单轮奖励上限为实缴本金的 15×。早鸟权重 2.0×、1.4×、1.0× 只影响奖励分配份额。The reward cap is 15× the principal actually staked. Early-bird weights of 2.0×, 1.4× and 1.0× affect each participant’s share of the rewards.

累计第 1–5 胜：当轮解锁 20%，剩余 4 份各 20%。累计第 6–10 胜：当轮解锁 40%，剩余 2 份各 30%。第 11 胜起当轮解锁 100%。Wins 1–5 unlock 20% immediately, with four 20% slices remaining. Wins 6–10 unlock 40% immediately, with two 30% slices remaining. From win 11 onward, 100% unlocks immediately.

后续轮次每次达标解锁对应份额，失败则销毁对应份额。失败轮销毁全部质押本金、一半当轮奖池和当轮团队额度。其余奖池滚入下轮。Each subsequent pass unlocks its scheduled slice. A failed round burns that slice, all principal staked for the failed round, half the round reward pool and the round’s team allocation. The other half of the reward pool carries forward.

连续 90 天停滞触发未解锁份额清零。奖池耗尽后，剩余待解锁份额走 30 天线性释放。7 天超时可触发异常中止，退回本金。v16 候选另含失败退款恢复路径。A 90-day stall clears outstanding locked rewards. Once the pool is exhausted, remaining locked rewards vest linearly over 30 days. After a seven-day timeout, a round can be aborted and principal refunded. The v16 candidate also includes failed-refund recovery.
